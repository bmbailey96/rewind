Live rental and purchase prices

US store prices are checked automatically through JustWatch. For another price source, connect a Watchmode key to add Watchmode quotes. Prices include store, format and check time. An unmatched film stays unpriced.

Sync Across Devices

Connect this browser and your phone to the same GitHub account to share tracked films, permanent hides, watched films, taste imports, streaming services, your budget, and recent Oracle picks. Make a classic GitHub token with only gist permission. Paste it once on each device. Connect the device with your films first, then your phone. Changes save automatically and refresh when you return to the site. The token stays on each device; it is never included in the saved film data.

Your taste

Import Letterboxd ratings.csv, diary.csv, or a favorites / owned-films list.csv. Ratings of 4 stars or higher, explicit favorites, ownership, and repeat watches guide recommendations. Watchlist additions never become taste evidence. A physical-shelf CSV can use Name, Title, or Movie Title; Year, Release Year, or Production Year; and Format, Media Format, or Media Type. TMDB ID is optional. Ambiguous titles stay unmatched. Copies appear as Your shelf; the shelf-only picker also allows labeled rewatches.

Your STATIC taste profile and published ratings are built in. No repeat uploads. Imports add new records; tracking records interest, not a positive rating.
